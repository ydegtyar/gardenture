User Data Deletion Instructions

According to Facebook Platform Rules and GDPR requirements, users have the right to request the deletion of their personal data collected through Facebook applications.

Gardenture: Mystic Garden does not maintain an independent external database containing your personal information. All game progression is tied directly to your Facebook Instant Games profile or saved locally in your browser cache.

How to Delete Your Data from Gardenture

To remove the Gardenture application and delete your associated gameplay data from your Facebook account, follow these steps:

Step 1: Log into your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
Step 2: In the left sidebar, click on Apps and Websites.
Step 3: Find Gardenture: Mystic Garden in the list of active apps.
Step 4: Click the Remove button next to the game.
Step 5: Confirm your choice. This revokes all permissions and disconnects the app from your Facebook account.

Requesting Manual Data Verification / Deletion

If you would like to request confirmation that any cached or associated records have been purged, you can email our support team directly:

  • Email: firstyuriy@gmail.com
  • Subject: Facebook Data Deletion Request - Gardenture
  • Information required: Your Facebook User ID or Player ID (found in the game settings or Facebook app settings).

We will process and respond to your request within 48 business hours.
