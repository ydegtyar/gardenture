Privacy Policy for Gardenture: Mystic Garden

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Gardenture: Mystic Garden ("we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you play Gardenture: Mystic Garden on Facebook Instant Games and other web platforms.

1. Information We Collect

Gardenture is a client-side 2D survival roguelite game designed with privacy in mind:

  • Game Progression Data: Unlocked persona skins, high scores, rare seeds, coins, and achievement progress are saved locally in your browser storage or through the official Facebook Instant Games Cloud Save API (FBInstant.player.setDataAsync).
  • Platform User Identifier: Through the Facebook Instant Games SDK, the game accesses your scoped Facebook Player ID solely to identify your gameplay progress and leaderboard standing. We do not collect or store your real name, email, friends list, or Facebook profile data on external databases.
  • Technical Data: Aggregated, non-identifying telemetry (such as screen resolution, framerate, and crash logs) may be processed in real-time on your device to ensure smooth rendering.

2. How We Use Information

We use the minimal information collected exclusively to:

  • Save and synchronize your gameplay achievements, upgrades, and run history.
  • Provide game features including card drafts, combat scoring, and hero persona unlocks.
  • Enable optional rewarded video advertisements via Meta Audience Network upon your explicit opt-in.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The game operates within the Meta Instant Games runtime environment. Third-party SDKs include:

  • Meta Instant Games SDK: For game container lifecycle, audio muting, and cloud save synchronization.
  • Meta Audience Network: For serving optional rewarded and interstitial ads. Meta may process device identifiers in accordance with the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because gameplay progress is saved to your scoped Facebook account via Facebook Instant Games, you maintain full control over your data. For instructions on removing the game and deleting all associated records, please see our User Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Children's Privacy

Gardenture does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly botanical fantasy gameplay with no gore, blood, or inappropriate themes.
